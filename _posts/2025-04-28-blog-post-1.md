No, this blog won't be generated in any shapes or forms by a Large Language Model. There's a place for that called social medias.

"Always take the long path, avoid shortcuts"
======
The main reason I prohibit myself to use it in this case (I found some uses somewhere else, which I'll share later), is that we substitue our thinking to a model developped by people who didn't use any, and with time it will have the same effect than automatic corretion on our phone, you will struggle to write with a pen double thinking the grammar of every words.
Another reason is that I'm more interested to share insight earned from experiencing, and while _"AI"_ _agents_ are now able to display steps taken to a solution, it's still lack the tinkering aspect that we can only experience while doing which gives some depth in a solution of a problem rather than superficial-yet-correct answer.
And last but not least, I enjoy sharing a part of my personality in the way in which I frame my thoughts, not just through the content but the form. At the end, isn't it known for centuries that a great writer will transcend a persona, whether his or her own or a superficial one, just based on the semantics.


“To play a wrong note is insignificant; to play without passion is inexcusable.” _(attributed to Beethoven, Ludwig Van)_
======
I'm still learning, do not expect me not to do any mistakes at the wrong time, or better, the right time. Do not expect me not to rediscover the wheel either, just do not kill the fun, it's my driver to excellence.
The piece of information encoded in failure is generally much more salient than successes, meaning you can't truely compete without failing a few times.\
In other words, I'm happy to fail, as long as I don't lose.

What can you expect from me?
======
I don't do empty promises, you already hear them enough in other places, but I will stick to post once every 2 weeks. Meaning every posts you'll read would have taken around 1 week to be thought out, tested, and another week to be written.
Would I be jobless and without any social life, I could reduce this to 2 days, but surprise surprise, I ain't.

I suggest that you do your own blog on your end if not already. At the end, anything carefully written that is coming from your own will be engraved forever in your head.\
If you do and would want to share it, feel free to leave the link from your blog in the comments below, I'll gladly read whatever passionate you, serendipity is always somewhere unexepected, by definition.\
*"If you are what you eat, then I only want to eat the good stuff." _(attributed to Remy, in Ratatouille)_*
