## A.N.O.V.A. – Automatic? No. Observe, Validate, Acknowledge.
In one of those lessons, we've been taught that nothing better says "I don't understand assumptions" than that one-liner F-test that you can use from that well known ANalysis Of VAriance.
Basically, it goes like this, imagine you’ve baked a pie representing **all the variability** in your data (Total SS). You want to divide it into slices for Factor **A**, Factor **B**, their **interaction**, and **leftovers** (error). But A and B slices overlap—some flavors belong to both A and B—so you need a rule for how to carve.
Yes, you might be surprised that this requires to select an approach because by default it will assume you're in one setting.
