In this project, we enrich real estate predictions by incorporating **geo-contextual layers** using open geographic data sources. 
The idea is to be the Bloomberg of Real Estates, and to provide a GeoScore for a house that would inflate or deflate its price estimate.
The system enables users to explore:

- 3D terrain overlays
- Socio-economic indicators (e.g., energy poverty, mobility access)
- Infrastructure elements like pylons, bus routes, schools, and Ravels (walking/cycling paths)
- Environmental zoning such as Natura 2000 or flood-prone areas
- Etc.

Multiple context layers activated, including:
- **Ravels** and **Bus** routes
- **Integration indicators** for vulnerable populations
- Natural features like water bodies and protected zones
- Etc.

These layers are integrated into the UI to inform not just pricing, but **suitability, risk, and value assessments**, providing deeper spatial intelligence to buyers, sellers, or insurers.

The stack includes:
- Leaflet.js for map interaction
- Custom toggles for contextual overlays
- Modular prediction backend connected to spatial filters
